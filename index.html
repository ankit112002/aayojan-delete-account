Aayojan – Delete Account

Use this page to request deletion of your Aayojan account and associated data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from an address you can access, or include your registered phone number so we can verify ownership.
  2. Use the details below.
  3. We will verify the account and process your request.
  4. You will receive confirmation when deletion is complete.
Email support@vkapsprojects.com

Subject Delete my Aayojan account

Include in the email

  • Registered phone number on Aayojan
  • Full name on the account
  • (Optional) Reason for deletion

What gets deleted

  • Account profile (name, phone number, profile photo)
  • Events you created and related guest / invitation data
  • Uploaded files and images linked to your account
  • Device / notification tokens stored for your account

What may be retained

We may retain limited information when required by law (for example fraud prevention, security, or legal compliance). Such data is deleted or anonymized when it is no longer required.

Timeline

After we verify account ownership, we process deletion requests within 7–14 days.

App name

This deletion process applies to the mobile application Aayojan, developed by Vkaps.
